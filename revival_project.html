Who We Are

Educating & Elevating University Students

The Revival Project is an Islamic society and charity dedicated to increasing knowledge and awareness among university students. Through regular talks, podcasts, and community events, we create spaces for learning, reflection, and meaningful connection — whilst raising funds for those in need.

📚

Knowledge

Curated talks and podcasts on Islamic topics that matter to students.

🤝

Community

A welcoming space for university students to connect and grow together.

💛

Charity

Fundraising for causes that align with our values and help those in need.

🌙

Faith

Rooted in the Quran and Sunnah, everything we do is guided by faith.
